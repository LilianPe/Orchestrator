# Notice

The method skills of this pack (`tdd`, `debugging`, `verification`, `code-review`, `receiving-review`) and the "load the method skill that applies" rule of the session-start hook are adapted, for the factory's context, from Jesse Vincent's **superpowers** skills: <https://github.com/obra/superpowers>. Parts of them follow the superpowers text closely. superpowers is distributed under the MIT license, reproduced below.

The review levels of `code-review` (passes per angle, verification of each finding before reporting it) are also inspired by the `/code-review` command of the `anthropics/claude-plugins-official` repository, under the Apache 2.0 license.
